Questions
parents ask

What age are these for?

Built for kids roughly 6–13 who spar or are starting to. The reps scale — a younger student does fewer, an older one chases the Stretch Star.

Do I need taekwondo experience?

No. Each card names the drill, the reps and what a good rep looks like. If you can read the card and hold a paddle, you can run the session.

Do I need equipment?

Most cards need nothing. A few call for paddles, a kick shield or BlazePods — they're marked so you can skip or swap them.

Is the app extra?

No. It comes with the set and tracks stars for as many students as you like — handy if you're a coach running a class.
