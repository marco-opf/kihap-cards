Privacy policy

Effective October 17, 2026

This policy explains how Kihap! Cards collects, uses, and shares information when you visit this website, contact us, or place an order.

Information we collect

We may receive information you provide during checkout or when contacting us, including your name, email address, shipping and billing addresses, phone number if provided, order details, and correspondence.

Stripe processes payment information for checkout. Kihap! Cards does not receive or store your complete payment-card number.

How we use information

  • Process, ship, and support orders.
  • Communicate about purchases, returns, or customer-service requests.
  • Prevent fraud and comply with legal, tax, and accounting obligations.
  • Improve the website and shopping experience.

How information is shared

We share information only as needed with service providers that operate the store, including Stripe for checkout and payment processing, website hosting providers, and shipping providers. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect our rights and customers.

Retention and security

We retain order and transaction records for as long as reasonably necessary for fulfillment, support, fraud prevention, tax, accounting, and legal purposes. We use reasonable safeguards, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we control. Some records may need to be retained to meet legal or accounting obligations.

Children

The store is intended for purchase by adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email kihapcards@gmail.com.
