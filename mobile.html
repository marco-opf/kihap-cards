Questions parents ask

What age are these for?

Kids roughly 6–13 who spar or are starting to. The reps scale with the student.

Do I need taekwondo experience?

No. Each card names the drill, the reps and what a good rep looks like.

Do I need equipment?

Most cards need nothing. A few call for paddles, a shield or BlazePods — they're marked.

Is the app extra?

No. It comes with the set and tracks stars for as many students as you like.
